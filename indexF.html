ℹ️ How It Works

Guide
MediChain Protocol

Five steps to ensure your medicine is genuine and safe.

🔐
Blockchain Registration
Every genuine medicine is registered on the blockchain with a unique code and QR before it reaches you.
📷
Scan & Verify
Scan the QR code on your medicine. MediChain checks the blockchain ledger in milliseconds.
📅
Expiry Date Check
After authenticity confirmation, enter the expiry date from the pack. MediChain tells you if the medicine is valid, near expiry (within 3 months), or already expired.
✅
Full Result
See manufacturer, batch, price, and a live expiry countdown so you always know it's safe to consume.
🚨
Fake / Expired Alert
If fake or expired — DO NOT consume. Report to DCGI Helpline: 1800-180-4444
